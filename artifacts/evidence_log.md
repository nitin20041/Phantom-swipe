# Evidence Log & File Search Strategy — Operation Phantom Swipe

## Search Strategy

1. **String search** across all acquired evidence files using regex tuned to
   the case: Track2 magnetic-stripe patterns, phone-number formats, GPS
   coordinate pairs, ISO timestamps, and fraud-related keywords
   (`clone`, `skim`, `fraud`, `payout`, `dashboard`, `declined`, `batch`).
2. **Metadata review** of the device config file (`device_config.xml`) to
   establish device serial number, firmware version, and first-boot time —
   used to build a timeline of when the skimmer was likely deployed.
3. **Communication extraction** from the phone's SMS log and email extract
   to identify contact patterns consistent with a distribution/payout chain.
4. **Transaction correlation**: card-last-4 digits in
   `local_transaction_cache.csv` were cross-referenced against the Track2
   data captured by the skimmer to confirm the cloned cards were the same
   ones later used in the online fraud.

Full machine-readable output of the automated search is in
`artifacts/search_log.txt` (generated by `scripts/search_artifacts.py`).

## Correlation Finding

The last-4 digits of cards in `local_transaction_cache.csv`
(`3333, 6666, 9999, 1234, 5678`) match exactly the last four digits of the
five Track2 strings captured by the skimmer, establishing a direct
evidentiary link between the physical skimming device (E-01) and the
online fraud transactions logged on the phone (E-02).
